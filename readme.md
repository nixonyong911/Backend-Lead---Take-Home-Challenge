# 99Tech Engineering Leadership Challenges

Take-home challenges for our engineering leadership roles. Your recruiter told
you which one is yours. Open that folder and read its readme.

| Role                                 | Folder                                             | Time budget |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Backend Lead                         | [`backend-lead/`](backend-lead/)                   | ~4 hours    |

**Please do not open a pull request against this repository.** A pull request
would publish your work where every other candidate can read it, and we do not
review submissions here. Send yours through your application instead, as
described under [Submission](#submission).

To get a copy you can work in, use the green **Use this template** button, or
clone this repository and push to one of your own. Prefer either over forking: a
fork can be publicly linked back to this repo, and so to your solution.

## The time budget is real

Each challenge is scoped to about four hours. We would rather read an
unfinished submission with clear reasoning than a polished one that ate your
weekend. Where a challenge sets a page or word limit, we stop reading at the
limit. Editing is part of the exercise.

## AI tools

Allowed. Disclose at the end of your submission what you used them for.

You own every line and every decision you submit. Your first interview digs
into your reasoning, and "the AI wrote that" is a failing answer. We filter on
ownership, not abstinence.

## Assumptions

Every challenge deliberately leaves gaps. Invent reasonable context and state
the assumption inline rather than waiting on us. How you frame a missing
constraint is part of what we read.

## Submission

You can either provide a link to an online repository, attach the solution in
your application, or whichever method you prefer. We're cool as long as we can
view your solution without any pain.

It is important that you minimally attempt every part of your challenge, even
if you do not arrive at a complete answer.
